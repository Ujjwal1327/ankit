This Privacy Policy explains how The Internet Catalysts ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or work with us on Reddit marketing.

1. Who we are

The Internet Catalysts is a Reddit marketing agency operated by Ankit Kumar, based in India. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at theinternetcatalyst@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use our contact form or email us, we collect your name, work email, brand name or website, what you're interested in, and your message.

If you become a client, we also collect what we need to run your campaign: contact details for your team, information about your brand, products and customers, and billing details.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the time of your visit. We use this only to keep the website running and secure.

Fonts

Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this.

3. Cookies

We don't currently use advertising or analytics cookies on this website. If we add analytics in the future, we'll update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

4. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk about how we can help
  • To prepare proposals and deliver the services you buy from us, including campaign reports
  • To send invoices and manage payments
  • To keep our website secure and working properly
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for automated decision-making.

5. Legal bases for using your information

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide services you've asked for or bought
  • Legitimate interests: to reply to enquiries, run our business and keep our website secure
  • Legal obligation: to keep financial records and meet other legal requirements
  • Consent: where we ask for it, which you can withdraw at any time

6. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, form handling, file storage and accounting. They may only use your information to provide their services to us.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, when needed.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or merged.

We don't share your contact details with Reddit or any other platform.

7. Client campaign information

Information you share with us about your brand, products and customers during a campaign is kept confidential and used only to run your campaign. Brand names are hidden in our public case studies unless you agree otherwise.

8. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact, if you don't become a client.
  • Client records: for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as we need them for legal and accounting purposes, usually 6 years.

After that, we delete or anonymise your information.

9. How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, such as access controls and secure, reputable service providers. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

10. International transfers

Some of our service providers may store or process information in other countries. When that happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it
  • Ask us to transfer it to another organisation
  • Withdraw your consent, where we rely on consent

To make a request, email us at theinternetcatalyst@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Children

Our services are for businesses. Our website isn't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

13. Links to other websites

Our website links to other sites, such as Reddit and Google. We're not responsible for how those sites handle your information, so please check their privacy policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

15. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us:

The Internet Catalysts, operated by Ankit Kumar

India
